# Printing poster_print.pdf on an HP DesignJet (914 mm / 36 in roll)

One PDF serves both paper types: RGB, 3 mm bleed, trim and bleed marks. The paper choice is made in the printer driver, not in the file.
These are general DesignJet settings, not measured for your printer. Menu names vary by model. If a setting is missing, pick the closest one.

## Always
- Print at 100 % / actual size. Turn off "fit to page" and "scale to paper".
- Paper size: custom, or "ISO A0+" / the roll width. The PDF page is 866 x 1214 mm; the roll is 914 mm wide, so it fits.
- Orientation: portrait. Auto-rotate off.
- Print quality: Best. Colour: managed by the printer, sRGB. Rendering intent: relative colorimetric with black point compensation.
- After printing, cut on the solid trim marks (A0 = 841 x 1189 mm). The dashed marks show the 3 mm bleed; red past the solid line is meant to be cut off.

## Matte (plain, coated, bond, "Poster" paper)
- Media type: Coated, or Heavyweight Coated for thicker paper.
- Expect the KU red to look a little duller than on screen.
- Dries quickly; handle after a few minutes.

## Glossy / satin / photo paper
- Media type: Glossy or Satin Photo.
- The dark red flats hold a lot of ink: give it extra drying time before trimming and rolling, to avoid scuffs and smears.
- Handle by the edges. Trim with a clean cutter.

## If the red looks wrong
Print the A4 test patch first (header red, logo, small text) on the same roll before committing the A0.
